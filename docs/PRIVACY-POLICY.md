# ChemVenture India Private Limited - Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

ChemVenture India Private Limited ("ChemVenture", "we", "us" or "our") operates the Green Paints website. This policy explains how personal data is handled when a visitor browses the website, submits an enquiry or quote request, contacts ChemVenture, or arrives through a marketing campaign.

## Information collected

The website may collect name, company, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, product interest and enquiry text. It may also associate a submitted enquiry with campaign context such as UTM source, medium, campaign, content or term, landing URL, referrer and advertising click identifiers such as gclid or fbclid when present.

Necessary technical information may be processed by the website and hosting environment for delivery, abuse prevention and security. Optional analytics or campaign-measurement services are loaded only after the visitor accepts optional measurement.

## Why ChemVenture uses this information

ChemVenture uses enquiry information to respond to product, technical, commercial and quote requests, follow up by phone, WhatsApp or email, maintain reasonable business records, understand enquiry sources, secure and improve the website, measure campaign performance where optional measurement has been accepted, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

## Sharing

Personal data may be shared only where reasonably necessary with authorized ChemVenture personnel and service providers supporting hosting, email, communications, security, analytics or campaign measurement. ChemVenture does not sell personal data to advertisers.

## Retention and security

Enquiry and business-contact records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for follow-up, relationship management, business records, dispute handling, security and applicable legal obligations. ChemVenture uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, although no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Cookies and measurement

The website uses limited browser storage for necessary preferences and enquiry attribution. Optional analytics and campaign-measurement technologies are blocked until accepted. Visitors can change their optional measurement choice from Cookie settings in the footer.

## Your choices

Subject to applicable law, a person may contact ChemVenture to request information about personal data associated with an enquiry, seek correction or erasure where appropriate, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a grievance. Reasonable identity verification may be required before acting on a request.

## Contact

Privacy requests should be sent using the privacy email and contact details published on the website. The final client-owned privacy email should be configured before production launch.
