About Green Paints

Powder coating expertise backed by ChemVenture.

ChemVenture India Private Limited was founded in 2010 and later expanded into chemical intermediates and powder paints for the coatings industry. Green Paints is the company's powder coating range.

The business focuses on product development, consistent quality, operational efficiency and customer value. Its supplied company profile also records customers ranging from job coaters to OEMs.

01 Development & Laboratory

Products are developed and tested before moving to the customer's facility.

Why Powder Coating

Efficient application. Durable protection. Cleaner process.

01

No solvent dilution

Powder is supplied ready for electrostatic application without solvent-based dilution.

02

Material efficiency

Oversprayed powder can be recovered and reused depending on the coating process.

03

Single-coat potential

The supplied catalogue notes that a single coat can provide specified performance in many applications.

04

Protective performance

Good mechanical, chemical and corrosion resistance with edge coverage.
